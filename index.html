How the Adventure Works

This is a fully virtual experience — no printed materials needed. View the full accessibility session catalog and add these sessions to your agenda right in the Salesforce Events App. Your attendance is what unlocks the rewards.

  1. 1

    Attend a qualifying session

    Visit any featured accessibility or AI ethics session. Your badge is scanned upon entry — that's how we know you were there, no paper required.

  2. 2

    Complete the session survey

    After your badge is scanned, the session survey will appear in your Salesforce Events App under Surveys. Completing it is a required part of the activation — share your feedback and key takeaway there.

  3. 3

    Post & unlock bonus swag

    Post to LinkedIn, tag @SalesforceAccessibility, use #Dreamforce26A11yAI, and share your top takeaway or "aha" moment (photo optional but encouraged!) to unlock bonus swag.

  4. 4

    Redeem your rewards

    Bring your scanned badge to the Disability Help Desk to claim your tiered swag during redemption hours.
